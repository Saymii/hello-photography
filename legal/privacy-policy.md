## About this policy

This policy explains how Hello Photography handles information when you browse posing guides, use offline pose recommendations, save guides, complete lessons, or change your settings. It applies to the Android version identified on the Google Play listing. The publisher above is responsible for this app.

## Information stored on your device

Hello Photography stores your chosen role (Photographer, Model, or Everyone), language, theme, reminder preference, favorite poses, collection names, recently viewed guides, and learning progress on your device. This information supports personalization, bookmarks, history, and resuming lessons. The basic posing library and rule-based recommendations work offline without an account.

The current app does not require sign-up and does not synchronize this information to a publisher-operated cloud service. Search and pose-assistant inputs are processed on your device; the offline assistant does not send those inputs to an AI model or API.

## Photos and permissions

The current app does not capture, import, or upload your photographs. Its bundled posing references include original illustrations and AI-generated images supplied by the publisher, with their source identified in the app. These reference assets ship with the app; they are not photographs collected from your device. It does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photo-library access.

If you enable daily inspiration, the app requests Android notification permission where required. Reminders are scheduled locally and may be delayed by Android's battery settings. You can switch reminders off in Profile or in Android's notification settings. Internet access is available for the optional Google Play catalog connection and links you choose to open; it is not required to read bundled guides.

## Sharing, feedback, and external links

If you choose to share a pose or feedback, Android's share chooser lets you select another app. The selected receiving app receives the text you choose to share and handles it under its own policy. Hello Photography does not automatically submit feedback to the publisher. Do not include sensitive personal information in feedback or in a public GitHub issue.

Opening support or other external links starts a browser or another app, whose provider may receive technical information such as your IP address. Those services have their own privacy policies. If you send a support request to our contact address, we process the information you provide to answer it. We use information you send to respond to your request. We retain support correspondence only as long as necessary to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion of correspondence you have sent. Your email provider and our email provider also handle messages under their own policies.

## Google Play and premium previews

The 48 original illustrated sample guides and the 39 available AI-generated photo guides are free to read, including four original guides labeled as premium collection previews. Advanced Academy courses show syllabus previews, while their premium lessons remain locked. These previews do not establish a paid entitlement. The supplied build does not complete a subscription purchase or grant paid access. If subscription product IDs are configured, the app can connect to Google Play to obtain product availability and localized prices. Google handles its own Play account and store information under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The publisher does not receive a payment card number from that catalog query.

Purchases will require a separately configured and tested Google Play Billing flow and secure server verification. Before enabling purchases, we will update this policy to identify the actual verification service, account identifiers if used, purchase-token and entitlement handling, retention, deletion, and service providers. No premium entitlement is granted by an unverified local switch.

## Advertising and analytics

The Google Play release of Hello Photography does not display advertising. Google Mobile Ads and UMP advertising/consent SDKs are excluded from that release. It does not include publisher-operated analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking services. Development builds can contain test-ad integration components; those builds are not the release described by this policy.

If advertisements, analytics, or new SDKs are enabled in a future release, we will update this policy and the Play disclosures to describe the actual information, service providers, and choices before those features are enabled.

## Retention, deletion, and your choices

You can change your role, language, theme, and reminders in Profile. You can remove individual favorites and collections. To remove all app-managed local preferences, saved guides, learning progress, and viewing history, choose **Profile → Delete all local data** and confirm. This also stops local reminders and returns you to role selection. The bundled educational catalog remains part of the installed application.

You can also use Android's **Settings → Apps → Hello Photography → Storage → Clear storage**, or uninstall the app. These actions cannot remove a copy you intentionally shared to another app. App configuration disables Android automatic backup and excludes app data from the supported cloud-backup and device-transfer rules. Device-manufacturer behavior and copies made outside the app are outside this app's control.

There is no Hello Photography cloud account to delete in this edition. If you contact support, send deletion requests to the private contact above. We will respond to privacy and deletion requests in accordance with applicable law. We may retain information where a legal obligation requires it. Local app data is not available to us to recover or erase remotely.

## Security

Local data is kept in Android's app-private storage. We request only permissions needed by the enabled features. No storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security. Any future publisher-operated service must use authenticated access and encrypted HTTPS transport before it is enabled.

A parent or guardian can contact us with a concern about information submitted to our support address. If a later release introduces child-directed features or data collection, we must assess applicable children’s privacy and Google Play Families requirements before enabling them.
